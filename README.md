## Project Overview

Developed a three-stage paid campaign strategy for a beauty brand covering Awareness, Discovery, and Conversion.

The project included audience segmentation, paid social media strategy, retargeting, Instagram Shopping, landing page planning, and conversion tracking.

## Campaign Objectives

- Build awareness for the beauty brand.
- Increase product discovery and engagement.
- Retarget interested users and encourage conversions.
- Develop a structured paid campaign strategy.

## Campaign Structure

The campaign was divided into three stages:

### 1. Awareness

- Instagram Reels Ads
- Instagram Story Ads
- Influencer Marketing
- Trending hashtag campaigns

### 2. Discovery

- Instagram Shopping
- Searchable profile keywords
- Audience segmentation
- Product discovery activities

### 3. Conversion

- Retargeting campaigns
- Flash sales
- User-generated content
- Bundle offers
- Conversion-focused landing page

## Audience Segmentation

Segmented the target audience into five tiers based on:

- Age
- Location
- Interests
- Behavioral characteristics
- Customer relevance

## Marketing Investment & Revenue Target

The project included a planned marketing investment of **$1 million** with a target revenue objective of **$2 million**.

> These figures are project targets and not actual campaign results.

## Landing Page Strategy

Created a responsive Unbounce landing page featuring:

- 20% discount offer
- Free shipping across Asia
- Call-to-action
- Product-focused messaging

## Meta Pixel & Conversion Tracking

Implemented Meta Pixel on WordPress and configured:

- Purchase
- AddToCart

## Retargeting Strategy

Developed retargeting recommendations using:

- Flash-sale promotions
- User-generated content
- Bundle offers
- Retargeting campaigns
